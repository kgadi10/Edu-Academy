About EduAcademy

History

EduAcademy began in 2015 as a community tutoring project and grew into a registered NPO providing tutoring and resources to learners in the region.

Mission & Vision

Mission: Provide quality, accessible education to underserved learners.
Vision: To bridge educational inequality through community support and technology.
